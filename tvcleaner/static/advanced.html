      Before you start

      • Use the TV remote to enable and approve debugging.
      • HDMI and ordinary TV USB ports do not grant cleanup access.
      • Android TV, Google TV, and supported Fire TV profiles use network debugging.
      • Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Roku, and other platforms need separate approaches.
      • Disabling bloat does not guarantee removal of every ad inside streaming apps.
